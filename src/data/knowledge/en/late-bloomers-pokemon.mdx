<Question>What are Powerhouse Pokémon?</Question>
<Answer>
Powerhouse Pokémon are commonly called "pseudo-legendary Pokémon" by fans. The group discussed here consists of the final members of three-stage evolutionary lines: they have a base stat total of exactly 600 before Mega Evolution and belong to the Slow experience group, requiring 1,250,000 experience points to reach level 100. They are not Legendary or Mythical Pokémon. In Poke Wordle, this tag also covers their pre-evolutions, so the tag does not mean that every member of the family already has a base stat total of 600.
</Answer>

<Question>Where does the name "Late Bloomers" come from?</Question>
<Answer>
"Late Bloomers" was used for a Pokémon Center merchandise collection. Its Japanese name, "大器晩成" (taiki bansei), describes potential that takes time to develop. The [Japanese collection announcement](https://www.pokemon.co.jp/goods/2019/10/191011_p02.html "Pokémon Center: 大器晩成 collection") uses this expression for these evolutionary families.
</Answer>
</FAQ>
